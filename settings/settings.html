Tidier

Tidier alone

This moves this browser from Sidebery to Tidier's own sidebar, spaces and folders. Nothing is closed until the record of it is written, and it can be undone.

    1. Disable Sidebery in about:addons. Keep it installed. (With it running, closing a folded folder's page would close the tabs in it.)

    To go back: enable Sidebery in about:addons first.

    Spaces

    A space is a Firefox container, with its own logins.

    Rules

    An address always opens in a given space. Text to find in the address, or /a regular expression/. Looked at for a tab's first page and an address you choose, never for a link inside a page or a redirect.
